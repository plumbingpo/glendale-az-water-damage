Storm Preparation for Homeowners in Glendale, AZ

Home / Blog / Preparing Your Home for Storms in Glendale, AZ

Storms can cause devastating water damage to homes and businesses in Glendale, AZ. Getting ready ahead of the season, and again ahead of each storm, can significantly cut both the damage to your property and what restoration costs later. Below, Glendale Restoration lists the steps every Glendale homeowner should take to get ready.

Seasonal Preparation

Complete these tasks before storm season begins: Inspect your roof for damaged, missing, or worn shingles and flashing. Clear out every gutter and downspout, then confirm water discharges the way it should. Look over every door, window, and exterior penetration, and seal around each one. Make sure the sump pump passes inspection and its backup power system works in a test. Go over your insurance policy, including what it says about wind and flood damage. Take up-to-date photos of the property to keep with your insurance records. Cut back trees close to the house so wind-blown branches do less damage.

When a Storm Is on the Way

Once a storm is forecast, tie down loose outdoor items before they can turn into projectiles. Close storm shutters or board windows if warranted. Ensure your sump pump is operational and the battery backup is charged. Important papers and valuable items belong upstairs or inside containers that are waterproof. Once the Storm Has Passed

When the storm is over, stay at a safe distance and look the building over for damage before you go inside. Take pictures and video of everything that was damaged. Cover roof openings with tarps if safe to do so. Stay out of flooded areas until someone has assessed the electrical hazards. Contact your insurance company to report damage.
